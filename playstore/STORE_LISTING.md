## Store listing (Grow → Store presence → Main store listing)

**App name** (max 30): `Bewosai: Business & Accounts`

**Short description** (max 80):
`Billing, stock, expenses & reports for shops, businesses and personal accounts.`

**Full description** (max 4000):

Bewosai helps shops, small businesses and individuals keep their money and records in one place: on your phone and on the web.

FOR YOUR BUSINESS
• Sales & invoices: make a bill in seconds, with discount and VAT, and print it or share it on WhatsApp
• Purchases: record what you buy from suppliers; stock goes up automatically
• Stock: products, services, units (box / piece) and low-stock alerts
• Customers & suppliers: who owes you, who you owe, with a full ledger for each
• Payments: record money received and paid; it settles their oldest bills first
• Returns and quotations
• Expenses: rent, electricity, salaries and more, paid by cash, bank, eSewa or Khalti
• Bank & wallet accounts with statements
• Staff: add staff with their own sign-in and choose what each can see

FOR YOUR PERSONAL MONEY
• Track personal income and expenses separately from your business

REPORTS
• Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Day Book, Cash in Hand, Bank Statement, Party Statement, Stock and more
• Pick any dates and download as CSV

EASY TO USE
• Sign in with a code sent to your email: no password to remember
• Nepali (Bikram Sambat) dates
• Sort and search every list
• Help chat inside the app with step-by-step answers

Bills printed from Bewosai are proforma invoices for your own records (not VAT/tax invoices).

Your data is kept securely online, so you can use the same account on your phone and on the website (bewosai.com).

### Data safety
- Does your app collect or share user data? **Yes, collects**. Shared with third parties: **No**
- Encrypted in transit: **Yes**. Users can request deletion: **Yes** (link above)
- Data types collected (purpose: App functionality, Account management; required):
  - Personal info: **Name, Email address, Phone number** (phone optional)
  - Financial info: **Other financial info** (the business records users enter)
  - Photos: **Photos** (optional, only ones the user uploads: products, logo, receipts)
- Not collected: location, contacts, device IDs, messages, audio, health, browsing history.
